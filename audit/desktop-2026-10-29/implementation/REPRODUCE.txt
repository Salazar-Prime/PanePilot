Run serially, with builds/tests complete. The core benchmark records its source
revision and environment. Renderer evidence here uses production source dfc14ec
and harness 9240cf4. Keep workload, runtime/hardware and host load comparable.
Every replay sample verifies the exact retained suffix. Heap/RSS changes are
end-of-sample observations, not peak memory or leak measurements.

services and a fake SSH executable that sleeps 200 ms. No real SSH/provider
archives are accessed. The same-request SSH comparison now calls the retained
legacy folder-browser helper and the new production async helper in alternating

used as the after measurement.

counts and retention are evidence; mock timings do not measure real decoding.

can commit elements before effects run. A superseded early-settlement trial was
replaced by the corrected run. 500 refs settle to 16 reads, three peak concurrent,
and 484 limit explanations. The unchanged PDF workload still records the known
snapshot retention/page-acquisition issues.

No watts, battery life, real SSH bandwidth, kernel wakeups or full-app speedup
was measured. CPU/IO are operation-level resource proxies only.
